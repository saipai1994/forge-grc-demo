FIRST LINE · SITE SCOPE

Owns business risks, actions, incidents, and evidence for assigned operations.

FUNCTION · MINIMUM ACCESS

Single-function user

Focused access for EHS, Quality, Environment, OT/IT, HR, Procurement, or Finance.

Can doComplete permitted checks and actions in their function and site.
GuardrailNo user/framework administration; employee health, investigations, and supplier-confidential records are separately restricted.
User access lifecycle
Request → approve → provision → review → change / exit
1 · RequestSponsor states business need, site, function, duration, and data scope.
2 · ApproveData owner approves; privileged/sensitive access gets independent approval.
3 · ProvisionSSO/SCIM maps identity to role templates; MFA and least privilege apply.
4 · ReviewPeriodic certification records reviewer, decision, and remediation.
5 · Change / exitRe-evaluate movers; disable leavers promptly and revoke sessions.
Segregation of duties
Incompatible actions need separate people or a documented exception.
Risk owner ≠ Independent residual-risk approver
Control performer ≠ Control tester / evidence reviewer
Finding/action owner ≠ Closure approver
Framework mapper ≠ Applicability approver
User/role admin ≠ Privileged activity reviewer

When staffing creates a conflict, record compensating review, exception expiry, and independent monitoring. Enforce in server-side policy.

Authorization and security checklist
Authorization combines role, site, function, record sensitivity, and action.
RBACNamed roles map to allowed actions; deny by default; no shared accounts.
Scope controlsLimit by legal entity, facility, department, record sensitivity, and assignment.
Privileged accessSeparate admin accounts, MFA, just-in-time elevation, logging, emergency review.
Sensitive dataRestrict health, investigations, personal data, trade secrets, and supplier-confidential records.
AuditabilityAppend-only access/change/approval/export/AI logs with actor, time, reason, before/after.
GovernanceJoiner/mover/leaver, access recertification, dormant-account checks, SoD reports, exceptions.
